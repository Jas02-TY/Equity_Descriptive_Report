# Section: News (Material Events)

Search for material news from the last 90 days. Use web search — do not rely
on training knowledge for recent events.

---

## Format
Open the section with: *"The following material events have been identified from the last 90 days ([start month]-[end month] [year]):"*

Group events under the following required categories (omit a category only if genuinely no material event in the 90-day window):

**Financial, Capital & Analyst Coverage** (financial results, dividends, buybacks, capital raises, analyst ratings/target price changes)
**Product, Technology & Operations** (launches, recalls, roadmap updates, supply chain, manufacturing, partnerships, leadership changes)
**Governance & Corporate Actions** (board changes, management appointments, ESOP, JVs, M&A, new subsidiaries)
**Regulatory & Geopolitical** (new rules, investigations, fines, licence changes, trade restrictions, sanctions, market access changes)

Each bullet:
- Lead with date: `[Month YYYY]` or `[Ongoing]` for events older than 90 days but still material
- Describe the event and its business significance
- Cite source: `(Source: news outlet, company filing, or earnings call)`
- End with impact tag: `- Positive / Negative / Neutral` (add brief qualifier in parentheses if needed, e.g. `- Neutral (mitigated by local production)`)

---
Example bullet:
`[April 2026] CATL announced Q1 2026 revenue of USD 17.7B (+52% YoY), driven by strong EV demand. (Source: CATL Q1 2026 report, 15 April 2026) - Positive`
