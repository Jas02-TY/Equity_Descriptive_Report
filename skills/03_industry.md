## 3. Disruption Threat Assessment

**(a) Threat Overview**
HTML table. Columns: Threat Type | Description | Likelihood | Earliest Warning Signal | Source

Rows (one per threat type):
- AI-related headwinds — competitive threats from AI-native companies, margin pressures; include earnings call management commentary
- Existential risk — single scenario or combination that could cause permanent loss of relevance within 2 years
- Low-end disruption — who offers "good enough" at 50%+ lower price
- New-market disruption — who is serving non-consumers the company is ignoring
- Incumbent vulnerability — where is the company over-serving or overpricing

**(b) Evaluability**
One sentence: state whether the industry's competitive dynamics are stable enough for 12–24 month forward analysis. Name the specific variable(s) driving the rating (e.g. regulatory uncertainty, AI pace-of-change, platform switching dynamics).

**(c) Under-the-Radar Risks**
3 short prose paragraphs — one per emerging threat not yet reflected in consensus analyst coverage or management commentary. For each: name the threat, explain why it is underappreciated, and describe the potential impact if it materialises. Cite source.
