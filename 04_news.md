# Section: News (Material Events)

Search for material news from the last 90 days. Use web search — do not rely
on training knowledge for recent events.

---

## Coverage areas (bullet points with dates)
- **Regulatory**: new rules, investigations, fines, licence changes
- **Legal**: lawsuits, settlements, court rulings
- **Geopolitical**: trade restrictions, sanctions, market access changes
- **Product**: launches, recalls, roadmap updates, patent grants
- **Operational**: supply chain, facility changes, partnerships, leadership changes
- **Financial**: earnings surprises, guidance changes, capital raises, M&A
- **Other material events**: any news that does not fall into the above categories but could materially affect the company's business, reputation, or valuation.

## Format
- One bullet per event
- Lead with date: `[Month YYYY]`
- State the event, its materiality, and direction of impact (positive / negative / neutral)
- Cite source: news outlet, company filing, or earnings call

## Recency rule
If an insight is older than 180 days from today, place it in a clearly labelled
**Historical Data** subsection, separate from current analysis.

---
Example bullet:
`[March 2026] Company announced a $2B share buyback authorisation, signalling management confidence in near-term cash generation. (Source: Press release 12 March 2026)`
